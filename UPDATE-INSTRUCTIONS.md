# Personalized October update

## Before you share
Extract this ZIP into a NEW folder and open monthsary-webpage in VS Code.

Open your OLD config.js and copy ONLY these four values into the NEW config.js:
- fullName
- birthday
- favoriteColors
- monthsaryPassword (digits only; use the day you said yes, in your chosen numeric format)

The supplied values are samples. Your actual full name, birthday, favorite color, and exact password were not provided in this request. Do NOT replace the whole new config.js with the old one: that would remove your new photos, captions, clue, and letter.

## Page order
1. Number keypad: clue is the day you said yes.
2. For my favorite person: uploaded photobooth photo.
3. To be loved is to be known: three questions.
4. First-chat screenshot: “Thank you, Lord, I replied to you”.
5. Happy 3rd Monthsary: uploaded restaurant photo and revised caption; no names.
6. Memories: registrar selfie and collage only, with your captions.
7. Your full letter: “For you, my baby.” heading; no signature.
8. Short Bible verse and cross.
9. Your supplied closing message.

All five uploaded photos are included, without altering their contents. The long story heading stays on one line and scales down on small screens. The first-chat screenshot displays in full; scroll if necessary. Existing theme and animations are retained.

## Test and publish on your existing GitHub Pages site
1. Save config.js and double-click the new index.html.
2. Check the password, quiz, all photos, both slideshow arrows, letter, verse, and finale.
3. In your existing GitHub repository, use Add file → Upload files.
4. Upload index.html, style.css, script.js, config.js, and the ENTIRE assets folder from this new project. Drag the folder itself to preserve assets/ paths. Upload extracted contents, not the ZIP or outer project folder.
5. Commit changes. Once deployment succeeds, refresh your existing website link.
